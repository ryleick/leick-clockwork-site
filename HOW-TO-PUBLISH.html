Getting your website live

Your whole site is in this folder — four pages (index, services, about, contact) plus styles.css and brand.js. Here's the easiest way to get it online and pointed at leickclockwork.com. No coding required.

My recommendation
Host the site free on Netlify, then point your Squarespace domain to it. Netlify is built for exactly this — you literally drag the folder onto a webpage and it's live. The contact form also works automatically on Netlify (that's why I built it that way).

STEP 1 Put it online (2 minutes)

  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
  2. Drag this entire leick-clockwork-site folder onto the page.
  3. That's it — Netlify gives you a live link right away (something like random-name.netlify.app). Your site is on the internet.
  4. Create a free account when it prompts you, so the site stays up and you can manage it.

Open that link on your phone and your computer to make sure everything looks right.

STEP 2 Point your domain to it (10 minutes)

Right now leickclockwork.com lives at Squarespace. You'll tell it to point at your new Netlify site instead.

  1. In Netlify: open your site → Domain settings → Add a custom domain → type leickclockwork.com.
  2. Netlify shows you a few DNS records to add (an "A record" and a "CNAME"). Keep that tab open.
  3. In Squarespace: go to your Domains settings → DNS settings for leickclockwork.com → add the records Netlify gave you.
  4. Wait a bit (usually under an hour, sometimes a few). Netlify turns on the secure lock (HTTPS) automatically.

If the DNS part feels fiddly, that's normal — Netlify has a step-by-step help page, or reply and I'll walk you through the exact records once you can see them.

STEP 3 Turn on the contact form (1 minute)

The "Book your free audit" form works automatically on Netlify. To get the submissions:

  • In Netlify, go to Forms → you'll see a form called audit.
  • Click Form notifications → add an email notification to ryleick@gmail.com.
  • Now every audit request lands in your inbox. Done.

TIP Making changes later

To update the site, edit the files and drag the folder onto Netlify again — it replaces the old version. When you're ready, I can also help you add pages, swap in a real photo of you on the About page, or connect a custom email like ryan@leickclockwork.com.
